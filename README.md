I trade fruit and veg at [New Covent Garden Market](https://www.newcoventgardenmarket.com/) for [Ortaggi](https://ortaggi.co.uk), and I write the software the business runs on.

Mostly that means sales automation, internal tools, and front ends that load fast and stay out of the way.

### Running in production

- **[ortaggi.co.uk](https://ortaggi.co.uk)**: the wholesale site. Every line of copy lives in a JSON config, so it updates without anyone touching layout.
- **[warmround.com](https://warmround.com)**: a studio front for the tooling below, generated end to end from one typed config file.
- **[emberhold.app](https://emberhold.app)**: an async study RPG. Focusing is the game, and your sessions move a world other people share.

### What I actually build

Sales work, for a business too small to staff a sales desk. A pipeline that finds buyers across a set of postcodes, scores them against food hygiene and company records, clusters them on a map for visit planning, and sorts every reply into an outcome.

Around that: a Discord operations platform, a capture-and-tasks PWA on Cloudflare Workers, a one-command project bootstrap, and a single-file nutrition tracker built for night shifts, because market hours and normal eating hours do not agree.

Most of it is private, since it runs a live business. **[training_insights](https://github.com/franko-c/training_insights)** is not: a Zwift racing dashboard doing power profiling and category prediction, built so I could see the trend instead of a wall of numbers.

### Stack

TypeScript and Node, Next.js and React, Tailwind. Postgres, Firestore, Cloudflare Workers and D1.

### Off the clock

Bikes, mostly Zwift, because market hours and daylight do not overlap much either.
